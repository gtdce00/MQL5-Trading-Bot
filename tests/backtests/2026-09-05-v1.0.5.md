v1.0.5 safety profile took one additional trade and activated the daily lock

trading resumed only after the next server-day reset. No permanent drawdown
lock or EA runtime/trade error occurred in this profile.

## Assessment

v1.0.5 improves execution safety across symbols, but does not improve the
EMA-crossover strategy. The baseline remains decisively unprofitable and is
not suitable for live trading. No strategy parameter was optimized.
Trend/regime research must define acceptance criteria before in-sample,
out-of-sample and walk-forward tests.
