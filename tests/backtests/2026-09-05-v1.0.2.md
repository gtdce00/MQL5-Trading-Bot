The unchanged v1.0.2 baseline is expected: v1.0.1 only separated the
daily and permanent drawdown locks, and v1.0.2 only rejects unsafe input
combinations. The v1.0.2 drawdown lock activated once at 10.00%, closed the
managed position, and blocked every later entry. v1.0.1 removed the repeated
daily drawdown-lock messages without changing orders or performance.

## Daily-loss circuit-breaker test

This is a safety test, not a strategy optimization. The same strategy was
tested with risk per trade and maximum daily loss both set to 0.5%.

- Daily-loss locks observed: 41 across separate trading days
- Positions were closed when the threshold was reached
- Trading could resume only after the next server-day reset
- No permanent drawdown lock was reached
- Net profit: -653.04
- Profit factor: 0.74
- Maximum equity drawdown: 779.27 (7.75%)
- Trades: 105
- Journal/Expert/Tester scan: no EA runtime or trade errors

## Assessment

The execution and risk controls behaved as designed, but the EMA crossover
strategy is decisively unprofitable in this sample. It is not suitable for
live trading. No parameter optimization was performed, so these results do
not demonstrate overfitting; out-of-sample and walk-forward tests remain
required after a strategy change produces a viable in-sample hypothesis.
